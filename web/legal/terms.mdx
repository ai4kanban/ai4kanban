### What you keep

**A Local board is not on Cloud, so a closure does not take it.** Its cards, memory files, releases and configuration are Markdown in your own repository, on your own machine, and they are the same files whether Cloud is on or off.

**A Cloud board is, so export it.** Its cards, memory, releases and history live in the workspace we hold, and a closure would take them with it. The app writes the whole board out as Markdown into a folder you choose, at any time and as often as you like, and that folder opens as a Local board. **The preview keeps no backups**, so an export you have made is the only copy anybody can restore from. Leaving Cloud is yours to do, and an owner deletes the workspace — both under Configuration → Workspace.

**The preview keeps no backups.** There is no daily backup, no point-in-time recovery, and no copy of your Cloud records anywhere except the live one.

**What ending the preview costs you is your event history** — which task asked what, what you answered, and how the run ended — and your Slack and Lark connections. Those are the things Cloud holds and nothing else does.

A copy of what Cloud holds about you is a data request to [support@ai4kanban.dev](mailto:support@ai4kanban.dev); so is removing it. A removal takes your whole account and everything under it, and therefore your place in the preview — the service has no way to remove one board's record and leave the account. It does not take a workspace you are in: your membership and your machines leave it, the workspace stays with the people still in it, and the handle you had is kept on what you already did there. A workspace you own has to be transferred to another owner or deleted before we can remove your account. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) sets out both in full.
