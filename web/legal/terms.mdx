### What your team keeps

**The preview keeps no backups.** There is no daily backup, no point-in-time recovery, and no copy of your workspace anywhere except the live one.

**Exporting your workspace is how your team keeps its own copy.** An owner can export a workspace from the app at any time, and the export restores a whole board on its own: cards, memory, releases, the board's configuration, its history, and its delivery records. An export taken beforehand is what survives a workspace being closed, deleted, or lost — and it is the only thing that does.

Deleting a workspace takes effect at once. The confirmed operation removes the workspace and everything in it, with no recovery window behind it, so your export is the only copy that outlives a mis-click.
