## A section

Headings from `##` down are what the "on this page" rail lists, and they get
their anchors automatically, so `#a-section` links to this one. `###` nests
under it; `####` is styled but not listed.

Inline `code`, **bold**, *italic*, and [a link](https://ai4kanban.dev) all work.
So does a list:

- One item.
- Another item, with a bit more to say about the thing it names.

### A fenced block

highlighting anywhere on this site:
