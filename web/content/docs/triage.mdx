**Triage** is the inbox button left of the bell in the top row; it turns red with a count
while items wait. Items arrive by themselves — from the **Proposer**, from `akb triage add`, and
as follow-ups a build or a plan notices along the way. An item is a lead, not a task: it is never
scheduled or counted until you make a card of it. To ask for work yourself, use **New task**.

Hover a row for four actions:

- **Make card**: plans a card from the item. If a card already covers it, the item is linked to
  that card instead.
- **Start now**: writes the card and builds it straight away, with no refining.
- **Discuss**: opens a discussion about the item; it waits until the discussion writes a card.
- **Ignore**: takes the item out. The toast has **Undo**, and **Record why** for a reason that
  teaches the board what to send you less of.

Tick rows to ignore several at once. Click a row for the whole item and a link to its source.

Two tabs: **Waiting**, and **History** — what became a card or was ignored lately. Click an
ignored item there to **Restore** it, or to record why you ignored it.

Triage is open to every account. Automatic sorting needs a Pro plan; without it you sort by
hand.

## Sort it

**Auto-sort** on the page, or `akb triage run`, judges every waiting item against your product
description and what you turned down before. Each one becomes a card, is ignored with a reason,
or is held for you when the call is yours. New cards refine themselves; a sort never starts a
build or changes a card you already have.

To steer it, record why when you ignore an item, and keep `project.md` and the planning memory
current.

### Let it sort by itself

On a Pro plan, new items are sorted as they arrive, so the ones worth doing are already cards
when you look. Each such card costs one refine run.

## Write an item from a script

```bash
akb triage add --title "What it is" --text "Its own words." --source "where it came from"
```

`--file <path>` takes the body from a file. `--source` names where it came from — `reddit`, `x`,
`xiaohongshu`, `weibo`, `zhihu`, `youtube` or anything else. An item already waiting or already
made into a card is refused. `akb triage check <source-id>` says where one already is.
