## What it does

As a few agents become ten, twenty, or more, assigning every task, checking every
update, and reading every output consumes your day. AI4Kanban acts as your AI project
manager. It organizes the work and keeps the execution details inside the team,
bringing you key updates, decisions that need your judgment, and results to approve.

Agents plan and do the work; you set the direction and approve the results. Two
workflows are built in: product development, reviewed by an agent before delivery, and
product video, made on your machine from a script you approve and captures of your
product. Other work needs its own workflows and agents added first.
AI4Kanban writes and produces; sending and publishing stay with you.

## More than one board

One repository can hold more than one board: `akb install --board design/kanban`
puts a second one anywhere you like. A board away from `docs/kanban` is named rather
than found, so pass `--board <dir>` on any command, or set `AI4KANBAN_BOARD` — a
command typed inside the board folder finds it. In the app the folder chip says which
board you are on, and switches between them.

## Where the rest lives

The commands are their own reference: `akb --help` lists them, `akb <noun>`
lists that noun's verbs, and `akb raw help` the bookkeeping ones. This site says
what to say, not what to type.

The flows themselves — how a card is refined, what goes into memory, how a
release is planned — ship with the command rather than with this site, so
`akb update` upgrades them all at once. `akb guide` lists them, and
`akb guide board` is how the board works at all.

The board app's own reference — what every column, panel, button and setting does
— is `kanban-ui/README.md` in the [repository](https://github.com/ai4kanban/ai4kanban).
