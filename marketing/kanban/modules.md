# Pillars

What this board writes about — one line per pillar, each led by its **bolded name**, then
what it covers and who it is for. A pillar is a theme that keeps producing topics.

- **kanban-engineering** — the thesis: once a coding agent implements a clear task well, the bottleneck moves upstream to deciding what to build and clarifying it. For builders who follow the AI-engineering argument and want a name for what they are feeling.
- **shipped** — a release, a feature or a demo, told as what you can now do. For people already running the board, or trialling it and waiting for one missing thing.
- **building-in-public** — running AI4Kanban on AI4Kanban: the numbers, the workflow, what broke. For indie developers deciding whether this survives real use.
- **landscape** — how the board differs from grill-me, spec-driven frameworks, other agentic boards and plain Jira or Trello. For people comparing tools before they commit.
