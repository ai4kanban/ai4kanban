# Writing

The voice every piece shares — one line per rule, in your own words, as
`- ❌ <what not to do> → ✅ <what to do instead>`.

Every rule here was read off writing that really went out. Nothing is invented. The lines
below were seeded from the nineteen posts in `social-posts/`; every later rule comes from
the diff between a draft and what you actually posted.

- ❌ Opening with what the product is → ✅ Opening with the problem or one concrete thing
  you noticed, and letting the product arrive late. "真正做 vibe coding 的人应该有共识，最头疼的
  不是写代码，是管需求" — not "AI4Kanban is a kanban board that…".
- ❌ Writing as a vendor — "users can…" → ✅ Writing first person, from what you actually
  did: "我自己拿它管着 200 多个任务", "I was managing 200+ tasks in my IDE".
- ❌ "Massively improves productivity" → ✅ A number with whose project and over what
  window: "在我们离开聊天框之后，产品开发效率提高了3倍". No number, no claim.
- ❌ A paragraph carrying three ideas → ✅ One idea per paragraph, and a marked list
  (🔹 💎 1️⃣) the moment there are more than three points.
- ❌ Only the good parts → ✅ Naming what it cannot do yet, in your own voice: "这个词是我
  自己杜撰的", "别吹牛了，真的很难".
- ❌ "Jira and Trello are dumb" → ✅ Saying what each thing is *for*, then where yours
  differs: "两者解决的问题有本质不同：kanban engineering 是自上而下的项目管理框架…而 grill me
  是轻量通用的决策工具".
- ❌ Dropping the repo link mid-argument → ✅ One link, at the end, after the point has
  landed.
- ❌ Ending on a link → ✅ Ending by inviting a reply — a question or a specific ask:
  "has your bottleneck moved to the requirement side yet?", "如果你也在做类似的事情，欢迎分享经验".
- ❌ Translating the Chinese piece into English → ✅ Writing the English one again from the
  same argument: shorter sentences, no emoji, no numbered-emoji lists.
