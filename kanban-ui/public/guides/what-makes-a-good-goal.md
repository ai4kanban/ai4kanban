# What makes a good goal

A good goal says where the project is headed, in your own words. Cover four things, in
any order: who this is for and what changes for them; how far out you're thinking and
what done looks like; what you already know you want next, in rough order; and the calls
that hold across features — what you won't build, and what wins when two good things
conflict. There is no template and no required length: rough and short is fine. The
board reads the goal fresh before every proposal, and it never drafts or rewrites it
for you.
