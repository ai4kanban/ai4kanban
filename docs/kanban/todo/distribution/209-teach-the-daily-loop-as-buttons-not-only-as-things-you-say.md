`docs/guides/daily-loop.md` teaches every step as a sentence you say to a coding agent. Once the app is how people arrive, a reader who has the board open in a window is told to go back to a terminal for work that is a button in front of them.

## Scope
- Every step in the daily loop guide says both ways to do it: the button in the app, and
  the sentence for the coding agent.
- The guide reads as if the app is the normal way and the sentence is the other one, to
  match how a new user got here.
- Where the app already has its own page for a screen, the guide points at it instead of
  describing the screen again.
- The Chinese README's link to this guide still lands somewhere that makes sense.
