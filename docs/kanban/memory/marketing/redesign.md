# Redesign

Design mistakes to avoid when writing a card, grouped by topic. One entry each: the
mistake, then the design we actually want. Read before writing or reviewing a card.

## Draft editor

- ❌ **A channel opens empty and asks to draft again** → ✅ one control at the end of the tab
  bar drafts and opens the channel, and its list excludes the channels already open.
- ❌ **Channel labels collapse to icons to fit the window** → ✅ keep labelled tabs and make
  the tab bar horizontally scrollable.

## Product positioning

- ❌ **Reduce AI4Kanban to task or context management** → ✅ ground its positioning in
  `memory/goal.md`: an AI project manager covering decomposition, iterative clarification,
  specialist design and the workflow through delivery. Spell the name AI4Kanban.
- ❌ **Classify by implementation form, or stretch project management to cover execution
  tools** → ✅ define categories by the responsibility users delegate, explain them before the
  listings, and back each assignment with evidence — a missing claim is not evidence of a
  missing capability.
- ❌ **Newsletter exposed bilingual copy and publication bookkeeping** → ✅ use English
  throughout, show the latest version and this week's delivered features, and omit issue
  counts and combined-version details.
