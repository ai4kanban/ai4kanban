# Redesign

Design mistakes to avoid when writing a card, grouped by topic. One entry each: the
mistake, then the design we actually want. Read before writing or reviewing a card.

## Product positioning

- ❌ **Reduce AI4Kanban to task or context management** → ✅ ground its positioning in
  `positioning.md`: an AI project manager covering decomposition, iterative clarification,
  specialist design and the workflow through delivery. Spell the name AI4Kanban.
- ❌ **Classify by implementation form, or stretch project management to cover execution
  tools** → ✅ define categories by the responsibility users delegate, explain them before the
  listings, and back each assignment with evidence — a missing claim is not evidence of a
  missing capability.
- ❌ **Treat Discuss as technical clarification or merely a UI upgrade** → ✅ Discuss helps
  users decide what they want; once that becomes a card, its questions resolve the details.

## The newsletter

- ❌ **Copy that lists UI details, uses vague metaphors as benefits, or exposes bilingual copy
  and publication bookkeeping** → ✅ English throughout, naming the capability and its
  practical value; omit issue counts and combined-version details.
- ❌ **Highlights picked from the last release or two, with one hero picture standing in for
  the rest** → ✅ draw the selection from every release no issue has covered yet, and give
  each highlight its own picture, without leading with their count.
- ❌ **A figure drawn as a lookalike of the screen it describes** → ✅ every picture is a real
  capture of the running product, cropped to the block the sentence is about; take a new one
  when none exists, and never stand a drawing in for it.
