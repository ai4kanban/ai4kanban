# What `content-writer` was corrected on

## Voice and claims

- ❌ Writing as a vendor — "users can…" → ✅ Writing first person, from what you actually
  did: "我自己拿它管着 200 多个任务", "I was managing 200+ tasks in my IDE".
- ❌ "Massively improves productivity" → ✅ A number with whose project and over what
  window: "在我们离开聊天框之后，产品开发效率提高了3倍". No number, no claim.
- ❌ Only the good parts → ✅ Naming what it cannot do yet, in your own voice: "这个词是我
  自己杜撰的", "别吹牛了，真的很难".
- ❌ "Jira and Trello are dumb" → ✅ Saying what each thing is *for*, then where yours
  differs: "两者解决的问题有本质不同：kanban engineering 是自上而下的项目管理框架…而 grill me
  是轻量通用的决策工具".

## Concrete value and actions

- ❌ Identity promises such as "work like a founder" → ✅ State the concrete value the reader gets.
- ❌ Vague actions such as "get to work" → ✅ Name what the agent will do or produce.

## Conviction and terminology

- ❌ Presenting an advocated workflow as "the workflow I want" → ✅ State it confidently as the ideal approach.
- ❌ Loose descriptions of task relationships → ✅ Use precise, professional terms such as dependency and lineage where they apply.

## Structure and links

- ❌ A paragraph carrying three ideas → ✅ One idea per paragraph, and a marked list
  (🔹 💎 1️⃣) the moment there are more than three points.
- ❌ Dropping the repo link mid-argument → ✅ One link, at the end, after the point has
  landed.
- ❌ Ending on a link or a question → ✅ Close with a statement or a specific, non-question invitation.

## English versions

- ❌ Carrying the Chinese piece's emoji and numbered-emoji lists into the English one → ✅ No
  emoji, no numbered-emoji lists, and shorter sentences.

## SEO blogs

- ❌ Filling an SEO blog to length with invented points → ✅ Filling it from our historical
  drafts, and only with points that are genuinely relevant.

## A second version of a piece

- ❌ Replacing the source's product category or central idea with a narrower feature → ✅ Preserve its key positioning and main argument when adapting it for somewhere else.
- ❌ Casualizing an adapted piece below the source's register — lowercase "i", "seen X?"
  shorthand → ✅ Keeping the source's register in full sentences; casualness reads as not
  caring in a piece that is asking for attention.
- ❌ Letting an adapted piece grow past the source because there is room for more → ✅ Matching
  your tone and the source's length first, then adjusting to what the place it is going
  normally runs — and staying short even where longer is allowed.
